# Michigan Constitutional Amendment: Public Qualification and Required Legislative Judgment

**Proposed Addition to Article IV of the Michigan Constitution of 1963**

**Version:** v9.1

**Current DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23548224

**Previous published version:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20689641

## Overview

This proposed constitutional amendment establishes four independent public-qualification mechanisms through which formally introduced Michigan legislative measures may become entitled to a recorded final vote.

It preserves legislative discretion over passage while establishing qualification thresholds, verification requirements, voting deadlines, and administrative accountability.

This is proposed constitutional language, not an amendment currently in force.
